I'm Tarun Mankar, an independent software developer, published author, and digital creator based in India. My work centers on the intersection of bleeding-edge Generative AI, high-utility Android applications, and deeply human psychological & somatic literature.

I build autonomous, modern software solutions that solve everyday problems — from intelligent finance calculation tools (SmartEMI, ReturnX) to viral comedy platforms (Jokewala) and offline media systems (TM Player, CleanSweep). In parallel, I research and author literature designed to regulate the human nervous system, conquer anxiety, and unlock unshakable personal focus.
