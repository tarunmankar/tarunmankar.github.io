Privacy Policy for TM Player

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Summary: TM Player is an offline music & audio player. We do not collect, transmit, store on servers, track, or sell any of your personal data or audio files. Everything stays 100% on your device.

Thank you for choosing TM Player ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Tarun Mankar. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how the App operates, what device permissions it requests, and why your data remains entirely under your personal control.

1. Information We Collect

TM Player functions strictly as an offline media player utility:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, gather, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment details.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign up, or link any social media profile or cloud account to enjoy TM Player.
  • No Audio File Uploads: Your songs, voice notes, podcasts, recordings, album art, and audio files are processed solely locally on your smartphone. We never stream, replicate, or upload your audio files to any cloud or remote server.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide high-fidelity audio playback, library browsing, and seamless background control, TM Player may request the following standard Android permissions:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Required to scan and display local audio tracks, albums, artists, and playlists stored on your device storage or SD card. Audio files are read solely to be played on your device and are never transmitted externally.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Permits the player to continue playing music in the background while your screen is locked or while you multitask with other apps, displaying lock-screen playback controls.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Required on modern Android versions (Android 13+) to render the persistent media player notification in your status bar with Play/Pause, Next, and Previous controls.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Used exclusively by our built-in Equalizer, Bass Boost, 3D Virtualizer, and Reverb effects engine to adjust hardware/software audio output to your listening preferences.

BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT

Enables smooth audio handover to automatically pause music when Bluetooth headphones or car systems disconnect, and resume when reconnected.

WAKE_LOCK

Prevents your phone's processor from sleeping while audio is active so your playback does not abruptly cut off.

3. Local Preferences & Data Storage

All playlists you create, your song favorites, custom equalizer presets, recently played tracks, and theme preferences are saved locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your device (e.g., Room SQLite or SharedPreferences). You have complete authority over this data; you can wipe it at any moment through Android System Settings > Apps > TM Player > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing independent development and updates, TM Player may include lightweight, non-intrusive advertisements served through industry-standard networks (such as Google AdMob). These services may collect pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies. We do not access, store, or sell this advertising ID.

You can manage or reset your advertising identifier anytime through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID).

5. Children's Privacy

TM Player is a general audience audio player and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since no personal data is collected from any user, we adhere strictly to international child privacy guidelines including COPPA.

6. Security

Because the App operates as a local utility without cloud accounts or remote servers storing user information, the risk of data compromise is minimal. We implement modern, secure Android architectural best practices to protect the integrity of your local preferences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any adjustments will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage users to periodically review this page.
