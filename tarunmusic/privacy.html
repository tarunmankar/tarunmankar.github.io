Privacy Policy for Tarun Music

Last Updated: October 01, 2026

Overview: Tarun Music: BGM Music (package: com.tarunmusic.app) is designed with deep respect for user privacy. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not track or sell your browsing or streaming habits.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe music streaming and exploration should be private. Tarun Music explicitly does not collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses).
  • Financial or banking credentials.
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Audio recordings or microphone inputs.
  • Contacts, photos, or personal device media.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

Tarun Music requests only the minimum technical permissions required to provide a seamless audio streaming experience:

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to stream music previews and background soundtrack metadata directly from our secure cloud delivery network.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & WAKE_LOCK

Used exclusively to enable uninterrupted audio playback when the user locks their screen or switches apps.

3. Analytics & Diagnostics

To ensure high app stability, minimal battery drain, and fast playback buffering, the app may use anonymous diagnostic services (such as standard Google Play Console Crashlytics) that gather aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs (e.g., Android OS version, device model, and crash stack traces).

4. Intellectual Property & Music Licensing

All musical compositions, cinematic arrangements, film scores, sound design, and audio recordings accessible via Tarun Music are the original intellectual property of composer and developer Tarun Mankar. The app provides streaming previews for creators and listeners. Commercial synchronization licenses are governed separately via official licensing agreements.

5. Third-Party Services

The app is distributed via Google Play Store and adheres strictly to Google Play Developer Program Policies. Google Play Services may collect standard diagnostic and device telemetry in accordance with their own Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Tarun Music does not target children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
